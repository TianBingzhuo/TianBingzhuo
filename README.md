## What I Am Usually Working Toward

- Embedded systems that actually touch boards, sensors, motors, displays, and timing constraints.
- Robotics/control projects where software, electronics, and mechanical work have to meet in one place.
- Experimental-instrument software, especially measurement workflows that need both code and physical reasoning.
- Open-source documentation that is honest about contribution boundaries instead of dumping every raw file online.

## A Note On These Repositories

Most of these projects came from courses, competitions, or team work. I try to keep the public versions useful without pretending they are polished commercial products. That means:

- code, wiring notes, reports, and engineering reasoning are kept when they help a reader understand the project;
- private records, course handout PDFs, personal evidence files, and unconfirmed third-party materials are left out;
- teammates' contributions are credited instead of being quietly absorbed into a solo story.

简单说：我希望这些仓库像一个可以被继续追问的工程现场，而不是一组包装好的漂亮标签。

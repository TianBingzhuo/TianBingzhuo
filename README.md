# Tian Bingzhuo / 田秉卓

Hi, I'm Bingzhuo, an applied physics undergraduate at Hebei University of Technology. I like projects where software has to answer to real hardware: motors, sensors, optical instruments, embedded boards, and the timing problems that appear when all of them have to work together.

My recent work spans embedded control, experimental instrumentation, PCB design, and open-source community building. I am currently following the operating-systems research route in OpenCamp, with a focus on learning how to turn engineering observations into reproducible measurements of communication, scheduling, and system behavior. Work in progress stays labeled as work in progress; I add results here after they are complete and reviewable.
